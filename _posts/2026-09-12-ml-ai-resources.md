## ML
> Would be good to go through CS229

- [MIT 6.3900 intro to ML](https://introml.mit.edu/notes/)
- [Stanford CS229](https://see.stanford.edu/Course/CS229/54): pretty math heavy

## DL
> Should go through fullstack, and d2l if 6.7960 not comprehensive

- [CMU d2l ai](https://d2l.ai/): most comprehensive college-like course, uses pytorch/jax, has exercises but no official answer key
- [fast.ai DL](https://course.fast.ai/Lessons/lesson1.html): fairly practical + implementation heavy
- [fullstack DL](https://fullstackdeeplearning.com/course/2022/): good for deployment + fullstack

## NLP
> Should go through at least Volume I and II of Jurafsky

- [Stanford speech and language processing](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/)

## RL
> Should go through 6.7920 or the Stanford textbook

- [Sutton and Barto](https://web.stanford.edu/class/psych209/Readings/SuttonBartoIPRLBook2ndEd.pdf)
- [OAI spinning up in deep RL](https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/user/introduction.html)

## LLM
> Come back to this after finishing all else

- [Stanford language modeling from scratch](https://cs336.stanford.edu/)
